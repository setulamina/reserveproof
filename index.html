A custodian can claim any reserve ratio. This makes the claim checkable.

A custodian registers and posts a bond, then commits each reserve claim on chain: the asset, the claimed reserve ratio in basis points, the URL of the auditor's page, a digest of that page and a digest of the audit engagement. Only that custodian can record a claim, so nobody can invent a number to attack. Anyone may then challenge a claim by staking GEN and giving a reason. The challenger does not supply the evidence URL — the jury re-reads the URL the custodian committed, so a challenger cannot point it at a page of their own choosing. The jury extracts the ratio the auditor states and whether the engagement matches, and deviation is measured against the attested ratio, so an inflated claim always fails. Every validator re-extracts and re-derives independently. If the page cannot be read, nothing is slashed.

How it works

  1. register_custodian — you become the operator and choose a tolerance in basis points.
  2. fund_bond — GEN is held by the contract, per custodian. Payouts only ever come from the custodian whose report lost.
  3. record_report — operator only. Binds the asset, the claimed ratio, the audit URL and both digests. There is no report, so there is nothing to challenge.
  4. challenge_report — stake GEN and give a reason. The bounty is reserved out of the custodian's bond the moment the challenge opens, not when it is judged, so a bond can only back as many open bounties as it can actually pay, and the operator cannot withdraw that amount for the whole life of the challenge. The operator cannot challenge themselves, and each report can be challenged exactly once.
  5. judge_challenge — the jury reads the committed URL, extracts the claimed ratio and whether the page explains its method, and the contract derives the outcome with |stated − claimed| × 10000 ≤ tolerance × claimed. Every validator re-reads the page and re-derives independently; a leader whose echoed outcome disagrees with its own numbers is rejected outright.
  6. settle_challenge — once. False: the reserved bounty is paid out with the stake. Sound: the reservation is released and the stake is forfeited into the bond. Inconclusive: the reservation is released and the stake returned, nothing slashed.
  7. reclaim_stake — the challenger always gets out: immediately when the bounty could not be paid, or once a challenge has sat unjudged past the 7-day window.
Nothing in this contract trusts the challenger's word or the operator's. The only inputs that reach the jury are the ones the operator committed on chain, and the outcome is derived deterministically from the numbers the jury returns — the public view check_verdict exposes that rule so it can be audited without running consensus.
